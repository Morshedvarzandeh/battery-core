Learning roadmap

An independent sequence of battery concepts.

Books, papers, standards, and public datasets may inform the work, but the explanations, examples, code, tests, notebooks, and visualizations are written specifically for this course. A lesson is marked available only when both its implementation and its learning material are ready.

Course 1

Battery Cell Foundations

Available now. Seven lessons from battery basics and cell structure through ratings, production routes and aging, with a final exam.

Lesson 1 introduces the cell components and charge/discharge paths. Lesson 1B moves through the structural scales inside a lithium-ion cell. Lesson 2 introduces the first quantitative battery-rating relationships. Lesson 3 follows a reference graphite/NMC production route through electrode manufacturing, assembly, formation, aging, grading, and final testing. Lesson 3B compares the substantially different oxide, halide, sulfide, and polymer routes used for all-solid-state cell concepts. Lesson 4 then connects cell structure and operating conditions to degradation mechanisms and observable aging signals.

Stated limits

What these models are, and are not

The production simulators use published process sequences and operating ranges, but their quality, throughput, interface, energy, margin, and risk-control outputs are illustrative teaching relationships rather than calibrated factory predictions. The aging notebook likewise uses simplified equations for trend exploration, not cell-specific state-of-health or lifetime prediction.
