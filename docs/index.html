Battery Cell Foundations

Learn how a lithium-ion cell is built, how it is rated, how it is manufactured and why it ages — through short readings, interactive labs and practice quizzes.

Offered by Lemonergy · Open source under the AGPL

What you’ll learn

  • Trace electron and lithium-ion paths through a cell during charge and discharge.
  • Calculate current, C-rate, ideal duration and energy with correct units.
  • Follow a lithium-ion cell from slurry mixing to formation, aging and testing.
  • Connect an operating condition to a degradation mechanism and a measurable signal.

Skills you’ll gain

  • Cell anatomy
  • C-rate calculations
  • Electrode manufacturing
  • Formation and aging
  • Solid-state electrolytes
  • Degradation mechanisms
  • Arrhenius analysis

How this course works

Learn, practice, track your progress

Learn

Short readings and interactive labs. Every lesson states its assumptions, units and limits.

Practice

Every lesson has practice questions. Numeric ones are marked in your browser with fresh numbers each time; written answers you check against a model answer.

Track

Mark lessons complete and see your progress on the syllabus. It stays in your browser, with no account.
