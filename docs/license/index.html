Getting in touch

Unsure which side of the line you are on?

Ask before you build. It is a short conversation and much cheaper than rework. Say what you are building, whether the code will be modified, and whether it will be distributed or offered over a network — those three answers determine almost everything. Enquiries go to Lemonergy as the licensor, not to any individual maintainer.
