Reaction roles during discharge

AnodeNegative electrode, where oxidation occurs.
CathodePositive electrode, where reduction occurs.

“Anode” and “cathode” describe reaction roles, so they switch sides between discharge and charge. “Negative electrode” and “positive electrode” remain fixed.

Battery literature often continues to call the negative-electrode material the “anode” and the positive-electrode material the “cathode,” even during charging. This workbench distinguishes fixed electrode polarity from reaction-based anode and cathode roles.

Simplified educational view: no voltage losses, concentration fields, SOC, thermal behavior, kinetics, degradation, or porous-electrode calculations are solved.
