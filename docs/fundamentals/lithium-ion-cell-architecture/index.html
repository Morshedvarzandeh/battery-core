Step 2

Look inside a composite electrode

An electrode coating is not a solid block. It is a porous composite containing active particles, conductive additive, binder, and electrolyte-filled pore space.

Current collector
Li+Li+ Li+
Show or hide each ingredient

Active material

Hosts lithium and provides most of the electrode's reversible charge-storage capacity.

Conductive additive

Builds electronic pathways between poorly conducting particles and the current collector.

Binder

Helps hold particles together and attach the coating to the current collector.

Pore electrolyte

Provides ionic pathways through the coating so Li⁺ can reach particle surfaces.
