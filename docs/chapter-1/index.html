About this course

Battery cell foundations

Seven lessons that take you from what a battery does and what is inside a lithium-ion cell to how it is rated, how it is made, and how it wears out. Every lesson opens with learning objectives, has practice questions and can be marked complete, and a final exam checks the whole course.

What you’ll learn

  • Trace electron and lithium-ion paths through a cell during charge and discharge.
  • Use capacity and C-rate to calculate current and ideal duration with correct units.
  • Compare a conventional lithium-ion production route with solid-state routes, step by step.
  • Connect an operating condition to a degradation mechanism and an observable signal.
  • Cell anatomy
  • Porous electrodes
  • C-rate calculations
  • Electrode manufacturing
  • Formation and aging
  • Solid-state electrolytes
  • Degradation mechanisms
  • Arrhenius analysis
  • Lithium plating

How to use this chapter

One question leads to the next.

Each part adds one layer of understanding. The two “B” modules deepen the preceding topic without breaking the main sequence.

  1. 01

    What is inside a cell? Name the components and trace charge/discharge paths.

  2. 02

    How is cell output rated? Connect capacity, current, C-rate, and ideal duration.

  3. 03

    How is a cell made? Compare conventional lithium-ion and solid-state production routes.

  4. 04

    Why does performance change? Link operating conditions to degradation signals.
