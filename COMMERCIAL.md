This costs nothing. It requires two things of you: publish the complete source
of whatever you build on it under the same licence, and keep the Lemonergy

Lemonergy holds the copyright, so it can licence the same work on different

## Which one do you need?

You are fine under the open licence — no payment, nothing to ask — if you are:

- learning from it, running it, or studying how it works;
- using it unmodified inside your organisation, including on an internal
  network. Section 13 of the AGPL is triggered by *modified* versions offered
  over a network, not by unmodified use;
- teaching with it, citing it, or building research on it, while meeting the
  licence terms;
- publishing your changes under the AGPL and keeping the attribution.

You need a commercial licence if you want to:

- ship a product that includes this code **without** releasing that product's
  complete source under the AGPL;
- run a **modified** version as a hosted or network service without offering
  your users the corresponding source, as section 13 otherwise requires;
- combine it with proprietary code whose licence is incompatible with the AGPL;
- remove, relocate, or de-emphasise the Lemonergy attribution;
- receive it under a warranty, an indemnity, or a support commitment. The AGPL
  provides none of those, by design.

If you are unsure which side of the line you are on, ask before you build. It
is a short conversation and much cheaper than rework.

## What a commercial licence covers

Terms are negotiated per case, and typically settle:

- the scope — which products, which entities, how it may be sublicensed;
- whether the AGPL's source-publication requirement is lifted for your product;
- how attribution appears in your interface, if at all;
- support, warranty, and indemnity, if any;
- the term and the fee.

## Contributions

Because Lemonergy dual-licences this project, it needs the right to licence
contributed code commercially as well as under the AGPL. By opening a pull
request you licence your contribution to Lemonergy under the AGPL **and** grant
Lemonergy a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free right to licence
that contribution under other terms, including commercial ones. You keep the
copyright in your own work.

If you would rather not grant that, say so in the pull request. The contribution
can still be accepted under the AGPL alone, and it will be recorded as
AGPL-only so it is never included in a commercial licence.

## A note on scope

A licence change applies only from the version that carries it. Versions of this
project published earlier under the MIT licence remain available under the MIT
licence to anyone who already received them; that grant cannot be withdrawn.
This page governs the versions that carry the AGPL and this notice.

Nothing here is legal advice, and this page is not itself a contract. A
commercial licence exists once Lemonergy and the licensee sign one.
